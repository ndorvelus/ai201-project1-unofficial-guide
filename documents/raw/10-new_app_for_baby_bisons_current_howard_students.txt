Hey y'all I'm a Howard student building
Bud
, a new social app starting here at Howard.
The idea is to make it easier to actually get connected on campus meet other students, discover groups, find events happening around you, and see what's going on around campus without having to hear about everything through 10 different group chats or Instagram pages.
I'm especially interested in whether this would be useful for incoming students trying to find their people and figure out what's happening when they first get to Howard.
I just put up the first Bud site and I'm looking for Howard students who want early access / want to help shape what we build.
Would love feedback too. What did you wish you had when you first got to Howard?
