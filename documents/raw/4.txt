What are some of the major changes made to the meal plan for the 2026–2027 school year?
The University worked with its on-campus dining partner, now Bison Hospitality, to replace the previous Traditional and Block plans with four newly structured options: The Crown Jewel, The Mecca, The Bison and The Yard. Unlike the 2025–2026 plans, the new plans combine dining-hall access with Mecca Dollars and MPBucks. The Crown Jewel and The Mecca include four guest passes per semester, while The Bison includes two. The plans also provide designated MPBucks that students can use at participating off-campus dining locations. The list of participating locations will be updated as new vendors are added.
Why were these specific changes made to the meal plan?
The plans were redesigned to provide students with greater flexibility in how and where they eat. The previous structure primarily provided weekly or semester meal allowances with Dining Dollars. The new structure offers a combination of dining-hall access, on-campus/off-campus purchasing power and guest access. The changes were developed with Bison Hospitality to broaden student options while maintaining dependable access to campus dining services.
What are some of the effects these changes are going to have on students?
Students will have more ways to use their plans throughout the semester. Depending on the plan selected, students may receive unlimited dining-hall access, guest passes, Mecca Dollars for campus dining locations and MPBucks for participating off-campus locations. This gives students greater flexibility to choose the dining option that best fits their schedules and needs.
What was the main goal on the administration’s side when it comes to the student meal plan for this upcoming school year?
The administration’s primary goal was to create a more flexible and student-centered meal plan program while providing high-quality dining service. The new plans expand where students can use their dining funds, introduce guest passes and provide clearly defined options based on students’ dining needs and eligibility.

Bison Dine Out Program
Use your Bison One Card to enjoy breakfast, lunch, dinner, or grab necessities at an off-campus merchant.
Students, faculty, and staff can use their Bison Bucks at participating Off-Campus Merchants.
Simply scan your virtual Bison One Card for payment at a participating merchant location. This does not imply that discounts are offered at these establishments when you present your Bison One Card. Watch for specials for Howard Bison on social media from our participating merchants.
