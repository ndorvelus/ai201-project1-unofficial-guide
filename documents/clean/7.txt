Summer Programs
The Bison One Card Office supports approved Howard University summer programs by providing temporary identification cards, meal plan access, Bison Bucks, and related campus account services for program participants.
If your department is coordinating a Howard University summer program and requires participant access to campus dining or other Bison One Card services, our team is here to assist.
Services Available for Summer Programs
The Bison One Card Office can support approved summer programs with:
Temporary Bison One Cards for non-Howard participants
Meal plan enrollment
Bison Bucks account setup
Laundry access through the Speed Queen mobile app
Access to eligible campus dining locations
Billing and invoicing support for approved programs
Temporary Bison One Card
Temporary Bison One Cards are available for participants who are not current Howard University students, faculty, staff, or contractors.
Card Fee:
$15 per card
Please note:
Replacement fees apply for lost or damaged cards
Current Howard University students typically use their existing Howard credentials or virtual ID
Physical card issuance for Howard participants may be limited to select program needs

Bison Bucks
Bison Bucks are optional, declining-balance funds that can be added to participant accounts for campus purchases.
Bison Bucks may be used for:
Participating dining locations
Laundry services via Speed Queen
On-campus participating merchants
Select off-campus participating merchants
Howard University students may also use Bison Bucks through eligible Grubhub integrations where applicable.
Laundry Access
Summer participants and eligible guests may use campus laundry facilities through the Speed Queen mobile app.

Summer Stay Guests
The Bison One Card Office also supports authorized summer stay guests residing in Howard University affiliated housing.
Summer stay guests who require campus card access may be issued a Temporary Bison One Card.
Card Fee:
$15 per card
Please note:
Guests should already have housing arrangements confirmed through the appropriate university housing partner
Replacement fees apply for lost or damaged cards
Laundry access may be enabled through Bison Bucks where applicable
Request Process
Support for summer programs and summer stay guests is available only for approved and authorized requests.
Program coordinators should coordinate directly with the Bison One Card Office once institutional approvals are complete.
Detailed onboarding instructions, participant submission requirements, and billing documentation will be shared directly with approved program coordinators.
