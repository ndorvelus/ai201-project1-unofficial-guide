Instructions
Download the app from your app store on your mobile device.
Open the app
Create a Grubhub account
Tap on Account
Tap on Campus Dining
Using the search bar type in "Howard University", then select "Howard University" in the drop down list
Answer a few simple questions
Enter your HU single sign on (SSO) credentials
You are all set to use Grubhub with your Bison Bucks account!
Atrium Campus Connect is the Bison One Card Portal
